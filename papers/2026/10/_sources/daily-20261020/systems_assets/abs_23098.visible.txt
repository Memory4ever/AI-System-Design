Title: InstAgent: A Recursively Self-Improving Agent Operating System for Heterogeneous Robot Collaboration
Authors:  Haoyang Luo ,  Junhao Qin ,  Jingyan Cao ,  Yi Huang ,  Guangjie Yang ,  Xiaoyu Chen ,  Haotao Lu ,  Tianxiang Gui ,  Ye Shi ,  Jingya Wang
View a PDF of the paper titled InstAgent: A Recursively Self-Improving Agent Operating System for Heterogeneous Robot Collaboration, by Haoyang Luo and 9 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract: Heterogeneous robot teams enable complex, long-horizon tasks by combining complementary sensing, mobility, and manipulation capabilities across multiple embodiments. Yet existing systems struggle to retain execution-time corrections as reusable experience, maintain task-relevant context memory across space and time for reliable handoffs, and explicitly represent the inter-robot dependencies required for structured collaboration. In order to address these challenges, we present InstAgent, a recursively self-improving (RSI) Agent Operating System for heterogeneous robot collaboration. InstAgent organizes team behaviors as agentic behavior trees (BTs) that explicitly model capability assignment, inter-robot dependencies and handoff checks. The RSI mechanism updates these collaboration programs from execution feedback and preserves successful revisions for reuse in future tasks, while shared multimodal spatio-temporal memory provides context for planning and handoff validation. Experiments on 20 real-world multi-robot tasks with three high-level models show consistent gains in task success, first-attempt completion, and execution efficiency.

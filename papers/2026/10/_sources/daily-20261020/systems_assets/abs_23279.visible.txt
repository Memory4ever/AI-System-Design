Title: Xiaomi-Lens-0 Technical Report
Authors:  Pei Fu ,  Yike Liu ,  Yue Sun ,  Hanmo Wang ,  Xiuwen Xi ,  Ruoceng Zhang ,  Shaojie Zhang ,  Chao Jiang ,  Hang Li ,  Bingrui Liu ,  Zhenbo Luo ,  Heng Qu ,  Changjian Xu ,  Ming Xu ,  Jian Luan
View a PDF of the paper titled Xiaomi-Lens-0 Technical Report, by Pei Fu and 14 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract: Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) answer questions about whole images well, yet they still struggle with fine-grained region understanding. A key reason is that text-only supervision never specifies which regions support an answer. To close this gap, we present Xiaomi-Lens-0, a 4B MLLM built on Qwen3.5 that predicts masks from its own visual representations. Specifically, a lightweight decoder combines vision-encoder patch features with visual latents, the language model's mask-token states, so mask supervision reaches the shared backbone without an additional segmentation encoder. Because each latent decodes to a mask, the model can interleave reasoning with region-specific latents and thus think with visual latents, while grounded reasoning traces supervise the regions it visits during chain-of-thought. As a result, across all eight RefCOCO, RefCOCO+, and RefCOCOg splits, Xiaomi-Lens-0 gains 5.6 points on average over Qwen3.5 in referring comprehension and achieves the highest segmentation cIoU among compared models. Moreover, it surpasses Qwen3.5 across all perception benchmarks. Homepage: \href{ this https URL }{this https URL}

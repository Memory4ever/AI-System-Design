Title: Towards High Semantic Fidelity: Hyperdimensional Symbolic Messages in Multi-Agent Communication
Authors:  Shoucheng Song ,  Chang Yao ,  Hao Wu ,  Youfang Lin ,  Sheng Han ,  Kai Lv
View a PDF of the paper titled Towards High Semantic Fidelity: Hyperdimensional Symbolic Messages in Multi-Agent Communication, by Shoucheng Song and 5 other authors
View PDF
HTML (experimental)
Abstract: Semantic fidelity is essential for multi-agent communication under non-ideal channels, comprising transmission fidelity and conversion fidelity. Yet existing numerical and neural message formats cannot simultaneously achieve both. To address this, we propose a novel message format-\textbf{H}yperdimensional \textbf{S}ymbolic \textbf{M}essages (HSM) and its corresponding communication framework \textbf{Herm}. For transmission fidelity, Herm suppresses semantic entanglement via an entity-attribute-value structured encoding pipeline. For conversion fidelity, Herm first leverages a receiver-centric ego-binding mechanism to align semantics. Then, a similarity-aware dual-branch fusion module is proposed to prevent critical semantic dilution. To realize explicit message-semantic conversion, we devise an iterative decoding scheme via algebraic inverse operations. Experiments exhibit that Herm outperforms baselines across various noisy scenarios while maintaining superior structural readability.

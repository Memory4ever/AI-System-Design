Title:LEAPS: Long-context Evaluation of Adaptive Pattern Learning and State Tracking

Authors:Howard Yen, Nitya Mani, Alex Renda, Christopher S. Hardin

Abstract:Long-context language models (LCLMs) power agents that act over long trajectories, from improving a codebase to running research experiments to forecasters that condition on lengthy histories. These applications draw on three distinct long-context capabilities: recall, where the answer is a fact present in the context; state tracking, where the outcome can be computed by performing a series of transformations to a specified state; and pattern learning, where the goal is to make a prediction by sampling from a distribution inferred from many observations. Existing benchmarks primarily focus on the first capability. In this work, we introduce LEAPS (Long-context Evaluation of Adaptive Pattern learning and State tracking), a collection of 12 novel datasets, spanning agentic workflows, behavioral modeling, and forecasting of market and world events.

A perturbation of the interface is semantics-preserving when the tool that solves the task is still available and still solves it, even though its name, its position, or the surrounding options have changed.\nL132: We want the agent to assign the same probability to equivalent tools under such perturbations, and CIPR trains for this in three steps.

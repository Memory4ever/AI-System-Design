 B.6 Efficiency and Computational Cost
Training parameters.
Because the Wan2.2 backbone stays frozen, the C2R# model trains only its
control and fusion adapters. Table  4 distinguishes total
model capacity from the parameters optimized during adaptation.
The training procedure in the original C2R paper fine-tunes the diffusion backbone
in Stage I, then freezes it and trains the added control and fusion modules
in Stage II; totals reflect the modules present in each stage.
Editto fine-tunes LoRA weights on an existing frozen VACE control branch,
which remains included in its total parameter count. The VACE 2.2 trainable
count follows its documented control-module training configuration.
The C2R# model optimizes 180.54M adapter parameters, or 0.63% of the counted
model parameters. For VACE 2.2 and the C2R# , counts sum both denoising
experts, although only one expert is active at each denoising step.

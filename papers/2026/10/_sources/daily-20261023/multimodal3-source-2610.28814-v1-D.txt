 Chunk positions and auxiliary action windows. 
The visual branch and tactile editor operate at 39.7 Hz and 119.1 Hz, respectively. Each visual update produces a new base action chunk. Between visual updates, the tactile editor uses incoming tactile observations to revise the unexecuted actions in the active chunk. The elapsed time \Delta t_{t,\tau} is measured from the start of that chunk rather than inferred from the tactile update index.
For future-pressure conditioning, the action window begins at k_{0}=\lceil f_{\mathrm{cmd}}\Delta t_{t,\tau}\rceil , where f_{\mathrm{cmd}} is the action command rate, and contains the next eight edited actions. A floating-point tolerance is applied at integer boundaries. Positions beyond the chunk are zero-padded and marked invalid. This auxiliary window-indexing rule is distinct from the loss horizon and from the temporal mask that marks already executed actions as non-editable.
 D.2 Evaluation Masks and Aggregation
 Physical hand-motion errors. 
The hand-motion evaluator supports a declared horizon H_{\mathrm{eval}} of eight or sixteen positions. A hand is eligible only if its complete evaluated prefix is valid; a truncated prefix is not silently substituted for the declared horizon. Predictions and targets are compared in the physical action coordinates used by the evaluator.

At prediction step k , wrist translation increments are accumulated from the start of the prediction window before computing the Euclidean error in centimeters. Euler rotation increments are converted using the xyz convention and composed by left multiplication, R_{k}=\delta R_{k}R_{k-1} with R_{0}=I ; predicted rotations are composed in the same way. The resulting rotational error, in degrees, is
 e_{\mathrm{rot}}(R,\widehat{R})=\frac{180}{\pi}\arccos\!\left(\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{tr}(\widehat{R}^{\!\top}R)-1}{2}\right)\right). 
 (15) 
Finger-joint MPJPE compares the stored palm-local positions directly, averaging joints 1–20 and reporting centimeters. Joint 0 is the fixed local origin and is excluded; the evaluator does not subtract a predicted wrist position from already local joint coordinates.
 Sequence-level aggregation. 
For each metric, errors are first summed over eligible hand–time entries within each sequence and divided by their valid count. Sequences with no eligible entries are excluded. Environment-level means then weight these sequence means equally. The EgoTouch macro-average gives equal weight to its five environment means; the Tachin overall score gives equal weight to its eligible sequence means. This aggregation differs from pooling all frames and should not be replaced by a sample-weighted mean from a different evaluation entry point.
 Contact-conditioned evaluation. 
In the contact-filtered protocol, a sample is admitted when at least one supervised hand satisfies the causal gate in Eq.  9 . Per-hand masks then exclude noncontact hands from action and future-pressure supervision. These exclusions define a contact-conditioned subset of the held-out data, not the complete held-out
test set. Retaining the base action for an excluded hand does not insert that hand into the metric denominator. Comparisons under this protocol require a common sample set, hand mask, and prediction horizon; results on an unfiltered test set represent a separate evaluation protocol.

8 Discussion and limitations
8.1 What transfers and what does not
The theory applies to any action checked against evidence by monitors that can be fooled. Whether the two
measured mechanisms carry over is an expectation, not a result. On the approve side, persuasion into fraud failed
on every pool we measured, so the approve-fooled sets followed the sources; where persuasion into fraud
succeeds, they would cross sources. On the reject side, what matters is injection resistance, a property of the
model that we expect, but did not test, to carry across tasks. Two Redwood Research preprints report that
monitors also differ in how far optimised text moves them in coding-agent monitoring: edited transcripts evade
some monitors but few evade a held-out frontier-developer monitor [ 33 ] , and catch rates
under optimised attacks rise with monitor capability [ 26 ] . The numbers
( 0.9 % 0.9\% against 7.4 % 7.4\% , Opus’s 30 % 30\% caution rate, the one payload that broke cross-cover) belong to one synthetic payment task,
its prompt and its pools.
Payment approval is a witness-mode task: its fields can be checked, and its sources (the invoice, the ERP record,
the supplier’s portal) are independent by construction. The results should carry over to actions of the same kind, each
checked against records the requester does not control: changes of bank details, refunds, procurement, access
grants and address changes. They carry over less clearly to judgement calls, where correct
monitors may disagree, or to actions with one source of truth. Judgement calls are agreement mode, which needs
Q 3 Q^{3} and was not measured. Several organisations that share monitors but trust them differently, each with its own gateway, are a further
question, that of asymmetric trust [ 3 ] , and are not treated here. The next test is a
different kind of task, such as
code-change approval against tests and a specification, or access requests against personnel records.
8.2 Deployment recipe
The measurements suggest one layer per failure. Against fraud, give the monitors independent sources, so that fraud
must corrupt a source and cannot simply persuade a model. Against obedience, use injection-resistant models, give
them structured inputs, and hide the agent’s free-text justification from them, which in the view ablation
brought every blocking payload to within 0.05 0.05 of the benign floor ( Appendix E ). Add a rule engine that compares fields and cannot be persuaded, which
in the confirmation restored the cross-cover condition. Against caution, which only measurement reveals,
approve on at least two independent sources, so that one source made to look tampered with cannot block a
payment, and escalate to a human otherwise. An illustration, assuming independent errors (not a measurement): put a rule engine
on the ERP record and model monitors on the invoice and the portal. Suppose an attacker makes both external
sources look tampered with, and each then refuses about 30 % 30\% of correct payments, as Opus did.
The rule still lets 1 − 0.3 2 ≈ 91 % 1-0.3^{2}\approx 91\% of them through, against 49 % 49\% under all-must-approve. The recipe
has a boundary: an attacker who corrupts two independent sources defeats it, sources behind one upstream feed
count as one, and chance errors of two monitors at once are not excluded. A rule engine cannot be persuaded, but
it can be fooled by a forged record, and then it rejects a good payment like any other reader of that record. An
attacker who forges one record and persuades every model on the same action leaves one rule engine on one
source, and the rule blocks. Liveness against that attacker, with b b forged records, needs 2 ​ b + 1 2b+1 monitors that
cannot be persuaded, on independent records, so that b + 1 b+1 of them still approve on b + 1 b+1 sources. The
confirmation’s combined attacks forged one source and persuaded some families on the same action, and the
thirteen-monitor pool stayed live with slack 1 1 ; an attacker who persuades every model at once is the case
beyond it. In AI control, trusted monitors are weaker than the
model they watch by design [ 19 , 29 ] , and which of them must agree is a question of
structure.
Why use AI monitors at all, if a rule engine cannot be persuaded? On the task measured here, one should
concede the point. If every source were a structured record with the same fields, three rule engines on three
independent records, the 2 ​ b + 1 2b+1 of the previous paragraph with b = 1 b=1 , would be safe and live by the argument
of Section 3 , and no model would be needed. The payment task was chosen because its ground truth can be checked, which is what made the measurement
possible. The general case is different, and each kind of monitor covers what the others cannot. A rule engine
compares only mechanically comparable fields, such as account digits and amounts. Of our three sources, only the
ERP record is such a table; the invoice is an e-mail and the portal is a web page, and finding the amount in
either is already a reading task. A rule engine cannot check an action that has no fields, such as a message, a
contract clause or a code change against a specification, and it cannot judge plausibility: a payment that matches
every record may still be a duplicate or go to a vendor that was off-boarded. A human can judge anything, and is
slow and costly, so humans are the escalation path. AI monitors are the only way to put judgement on every action
at scale, and they are the ones that can be persuaded. The recipe therefore uses all three, and the rule of
Section 3 says how to combine them: a rule engine added to a vote of twelve is outvoted by a
persuaded majority, while “approve on two sources” uses it at full strength.
8.3 Limits
Residual risk.
Our guarantees hold when the realised fooled set lies in the assumed family for its
direction. The directional kernel approves on two monitors from different sources, so one stray approval outside the
poisoned column suffices for an unsafe approval. That happened on 0.001 0.001 of the confirmation’s fraud items
and never on the frontier pool. The margin-2 kernel removes it at 13.2 % 13.2\% false rejections. For
benign errors statistical aggregation is appropriate [ 30 , 25 ] . For adversarial
ones, a set property is.
Illegitimate requests (out of scope, not evaluated).
An action that is correctly formed but should not
exist needs an authorisation layer: authenticated requests, two-person rules, payee allow-lists. Monitors reading
records of intent , such as purchase orders, would extend “diversify the inputs” to it (untested).
External validity.
The small monitors are four 4-bit quantised open-weights models of 4 4 – 9 9 B
parameters. The frontier-class ones are fixed only by their identifiers on the day, mostly at the lowest
reasoning setting. We tested one or two models per provider (not Claude Fable). We used one synthetic task family and one attacker model per small-pool study. The prompt was
fixed on the small models on unattacked items, which plausibly created their clean approve side
( Appendix B ). Only Opus showed caution, and only Opus deliberated on those items ( Table 17 ), so model
and deliberation are confounded.
What we do not claim.
We claim no novelty for fail-prone systems over monitors, for Q 2 Q^{2} and
Q 3 Q^{3} , for the gap between weighted and structured rules, or for the cross-cover condition. We do not claim that
real pools have the grid structure of Section 2 . Every measured approve side has columns and no rows. The
worst-case claim for the twelve language-model monitors failed. Opus’s resistance to persuasion is bounded only
against two writers and about $ 76 \$76 of search. Other attackers persuade other
monitors [ 26 , 33 ] and, two preprints report, commercial
guardrails [ 59 , 21 ] . False-rejection attacks have been reported. We add their set structure
across a pool.
9 Conclusion
Diversify the inputs, harden the monitors, count sources, not votes, and include a monitor that cannot be
persuaded. Fooling into fraud followed the sources on every pool measured. Fooling into blocking followed injection
resistance, which size did not predict. Counting sources blocked 0.9 % 0.9\% of correct payments against 7.4 % 7.4\% at
the same safety, as pre-registered. The theory said where this breaks, and the confirmation found it. The
worst-case claim for twelve language-model monitors is a negative result, and a rule engine restores the
condition. All of it is measured on payment approval. Every zero bounds only the attacks we ran.
Ethics
All targets are our own monitor deployments on fictional tasks. No real payment is made, and no real organisation
is imitated. Payloads reuse no harmful behaviours from jailbreak collections. The small monitors and the pilot’s
attacker ran locally. The frontier arms sent the same fictional items to four providers’ APIs, under their usage
terms and spending caps. In the tailored-attack arms, models were also asked to rewrite a note as the attacker,
through the same APIs and on the same fictional data. Refusals were recorded, and the prompt was not reworded to
get around them.

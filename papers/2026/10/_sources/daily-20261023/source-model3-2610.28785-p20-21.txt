D.5 ELIGIBILITY AND VERIFIER CHECKS
Exclusion affects training allocation only. The 104 excluded tasks comprise 66 initially satisfied
verifiers, 33 objectives ruled out by an initial-state guard proof, and 5 tasks identified by a string-
matching heuristic. The final category is not a proof-based certificate. The guard does not fire on any
of the826bank tasks solved at least once. Evaluation and validation pools remain unchanged.
A verifier that already accepts the initial state does not establish meaningful task completion by a new
trajectory. Conversely, repeated observed failures do not prove that success is impossible. Structural
checks and posterior evidence therefore serve different roles: the checks determine eligibility, and the
posterior ranks and weights eligible tasks. The non-degeneracy objective in Section 4.1 motivates
weighting both consistently successful and consistently unsuccessful tasks below tasks likely to
produce mixed rewards.

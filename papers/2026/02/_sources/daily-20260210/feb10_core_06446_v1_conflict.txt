L390@P4: 5.3 Semantic Collapse Rate
L391@P4: Semantic collapse rate (proportion of unrelated
L392@P4: pairs misclassified as having relations) averages
L393@P4: 37.6% across models, substantially lower than
L394@P4: random guessing's expected 75% error. This
L395@P4: indicates models do not fail through random
L396@P4: guessing but through systematic generation of false
L397@P4: relational structures (Liu et al., 2022), a known
L398@P4: pathology in neural language models.

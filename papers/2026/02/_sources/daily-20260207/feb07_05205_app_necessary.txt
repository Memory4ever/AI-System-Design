818: Appendix A Additional Details on Dataset Creation
819: In WikiSQE, the same sentence may appear under multiple labels.
820: For pairwise construction we avoid pairing identical sentences with themselves; however, if duplicates occur across different categories, this is not problematic for our task, whereas duplicates within the same category group are filtered out.
821: When assembling the data, we sample within each category so that counts are as balanced as possible across labels; if a label is underrepresented, we compensate by sampling from labels with larger pools.
822: Although malformed sentences were discarded during annotation, the number of pairs per category after filtering is reported in Table 6.
823: Finally, although LLMs are known to exhibit option–position bias (Li and Gao, 2025), in our setup the correct option is not fixed to a particular position, so this bias is not a concern.

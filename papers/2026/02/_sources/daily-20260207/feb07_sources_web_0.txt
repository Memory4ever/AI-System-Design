L16: 
L17: The Alignment team works to understand the risks of AI models and develop ways to ensure that future ones remain helpful, honest, and harmless.

L23: ### Frontier Red Team
L24: 
L25: The Frontier Red Team analyzes the implications of frontier AI models for cybersecurity, biosecurity, and autonomous systems.
L26: ### Interpretability
L27: 
L28: The mission of the Interpretability team is to understand how large language models work internally, as a foundation for AI safety and positive outcomes.

1463: Appendix B Experimental Details
1464: B.1 PRM Selection for Reasoning Step Reward
1465: In Section 4.1, we discussed the utilization of PRM to evaluate the faithfulness of CoT sentences relative to the context. To identify the optimal model for this judgment task, we comparatively evaluated Qwen3-8B (Yang et al., 2025) and HHEM-2.1 (Hughes et al., 2023) as the underlying PRM. We then conducte hallucination mitigation experiments using our proposed method with both configurations.
1466: From Table 8, it can be seen that using HHEM-2.1 as the PRM achieved good results, with high Acc. and Faithful Rate. Therefore, we chose HHEM-2.1 as the PRM for the experiment.
1467: B.2 Baseline Details
1468: While FaithRL begins with 8 initial rollouts, its resampling strategy effectively results in 16 samples per prompt. To ensure a fair comparison, we generate 16 rollouts per prompt for all baselines, matching the total number of samples produced by FaithRL’s resampling strategy.
1469: SFT.
1470: For a fair comparison, we use the same training set as FaithRL. For each question in the training set, we sample 16 responses and select those with correct answers as the final SFT training set.
1471: KD (Nguyen et al., 2025).
1472: We apply knowledge distillation to improve factuality, using Qwen3-32B and DPSK-32B as teachers for their respective student models Qwen3 series and DPSK-1.5B. Following  Nguyen et al. (2025), we maintain identical hyperparameters and settings. To ensure fairness in data quality, we adopt a rejection sampling approach where 16 responses are generated per training instance. The sample that satisfies the correctness criteria and yields the maximum F1 score is designated as the hard label for student training.
1473: Self-Refine (Madaan et al., 2023).
1474: Generate the initial response, evaluate it through feedback, and optimize the response iteratively based on this feedback until a satisfactory version is obtained. We follow  Madaan et al. (2023) and set the maximum number of iterations as 3.
1475: GRPO (Shao et al., 2024).
1476: Follow Shao et al. (2024), we use the original GRPO strategy with only outcome correctness as the reward signal. For a fair comparison, except for the rollout count being 16, the hyperparameter settings of GRPO are the same as those of FaithRL.
1477: FSPO (Li and Ng, 2025).
1478: By assessing the reasoning trajectory through fine-grained step-wise rewards, FSPO fosters a more robust reliance on CoT, thereby mitigating grounding errors. To remain consistent with FaithRL, we modified the FSPO prompt structure so that the model has access to the full context from the initial question, maintaining structural consistency with our approach. For a fair comparison, except for the rollout count being 16, the hyperparameter settings of FSPO are the same as those of FaithRL.

L375: We train the teacher model for five epochs and select the final matrix that minimizes $\mathcal{L}_{\text{grad}}$ on the premise of preserving the teacher’s original performance. L376: Following established practices in prior distillation studies (cite44†Wang et al., 2025 ; cite37†Gu et al., 2024 ; cite94†Ko et al., 2024 ), we search distillation hyperparameters within their recommended ranges to reach better performance under clean distillation while adopting results with more prominent defense effectiveness. Specifically, we vary the number of training epochs in {1, 2, 5}.
L377: The learning rate is selected from {1e-6, 5e-6, 1e-5, 5e-5} for the Qwen family and {1e-5, 5e-5, 1e-4} for the Llama family. All models are optimized using the AdamW optimizer with a weight decay of $0.01$ and momentum coefficients $\beta_{1}=0.9$ and $\beta_{2}=0.999$. The learning rate schedule consists of a linear warm-up over the first 10% of training steps, followed by cosine annealing to zero.
L378: We adopt greedy decoding for all experiments for stable and reproducible evaluations. The max new token is set to 512 by default, and is 2048 for the MATH-500 benchmark as it requires a substantially longer reasoning process.

L441: As observed, the defense leads to an average accuracy drop of $8.79\%$ across various distillation algorithms and surrogate students on GSM8K, which significantly exceeds the drop of $4.52\%$ observed on black-box students, as the transformation matrix is directly optimized against these surrogate models.

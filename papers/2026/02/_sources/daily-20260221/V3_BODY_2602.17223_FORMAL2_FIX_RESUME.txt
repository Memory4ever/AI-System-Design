Appendix F Training Details for Protocol 2
In this section we provide further details for the experiments conducted in Section 5.3.
We train on the FineWeb-Edu dataset (Lozhkov et al., 2024). This is a large-scale dataset of 1.3T total tokens consisting of high-quality educational web pages filtered from the larger FineWeb dataset. This dataset has been used for pretraining, and is suitable for general testing of language modeling capabilities of LLMs. We take 40000 samples from this dataset and divide these into an 80/20 train-validation split. We perform training for 500 steps with a batch size of 64 on sequences of length 256. We use the optimizer AdamW (Loshchilov and Hutter, 2019) with a learning rate of 5​e−45e-4, with no warmup steps. The base model is Llama-3.2-1B, and the weights of this model are frozen; gradients are backpropagated through this model in order to reach the NoiseEmbedder module.
We utilize the same sampled noise at every token position, but a different noise is sampled for each batch element. Our results are reported using a λ\lambda hyperparameter value of 3.5. We train using an A100 GPU.

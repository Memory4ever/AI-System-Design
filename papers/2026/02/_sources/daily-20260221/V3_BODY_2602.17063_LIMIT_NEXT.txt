6 Limitations
Our study has several limitations. (i) We also provide practical implementation of sign lock-in to sub-bit compression in Appendix E.3, but there is substantial room for future study.
(ii) While sign lock-in typically emerges under natural training dynamics, the system can be driven into a sign floating mode
under extraordinarily strong magnitude-side regularization that induces persistent attraction toward the sign boundary. We also analyze sign floating mode in Appendix D.6.
(iii) We focus on simple enforcement methods;
other strategies
remain unexplored.
(iv) We have conducted extensive experiments including billion-scale experiments, but a wider validation is future work.
(v) While we show that sign degrees of
freedom are rarely exploited by optimization, we do not analyze
the representational role of signs when treated as fixed
parameters, nor their potential contribution to expressivity.

4 Passive Evaluator Lower Bounds
Intuition.
If triggers occur with probability ε\varepsilon under 𝒟eval\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{eval}},
then mm i.i.d. samples encounter at least one trigger with probability 1−(1−ε)m1-(1-\varepsilon)^{m}.
When m​ε≪1m\varepsilon\ll 1, almost all samples come from the non-trigger region, where safe
and unsafe models produce identical outputs. The evaluator thus observes statistically
identical transcripts for both model types, and cannot reliably distinguish them.

5 Adaptive Evaluator Hardness
5.1 Setup
The key insight is that an adaptive evaluator’s advantage comes from choosing queries
based on past responses. An mm-wise independent hash construction eliminates this
advantage: every query, however chosen, hits the trigger set with the same probability ε\varepsilon.
Model class construction.

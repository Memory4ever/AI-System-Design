The main benefits of our proposed Procrustes alignment are two-fold.
First, it resolves the issue due to non-unique decomposition. In particular, the Procrustes alignment approach selects, among all valid decompositions of 𝐐t+1\mathbf{Q}^{t+1}, the one which is nearest to that in the last fine-tuning round (i.e., 𝐀t\mathbf{A}^{t}) in Frobenius norm.

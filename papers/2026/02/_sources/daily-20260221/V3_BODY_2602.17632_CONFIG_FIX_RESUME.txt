L.1 IQL
IQL learns three networks a critic QϕQ_{\phi}, value VψV_{\psi}, and policy πθ\pi_{\theta}. The IQL loss functions are as follows:
ℒ⁡(ϕ)=𝔼s,a,r,s′∼𝒟​[(Qϕ​(s,a)−r−γ​Vψ​(s′))2]\mathcal{L}(\phi)=\mathbb{E}_{s,a,r,s^{\prime}\sim\mathcal{D}}[(Q_{\phi}(s,a)-r-\gamma V_{\psi}(s^{\prime}))^{2}]
ℒ⁡(ψ)=𝔼s∼𝒟,a′∼πθ​(a|s)​[|τ−δ⁡(Vψ​(s)−Qϕ​(s,a)<0)|​(Vψ​(s)−Qϕ​(s,a))2]\mathcal{L}(\psi)=\mathbb{E}_{s\sim\mathcal{D},a^{\prime}\sim\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}[|\tau-\delta(V_{\psi}(s)-Q_{\phi}(s,a)<0)|(V_{\psi}(s)-Q_{\phi}(s,a))^{2}]
The term |τ−δ⁡(Vψ​(s)−Qϕ​(s,a)<0)||\tau-\delta(V_{\psi}(s)-Q_{\phi}(s,a)<0)| is the expectile loss so differences where Vψ​(s)−Qϕ​(s,a)>0V_{\psi}(s)-Q_{\phi}(s,a)>0 have τ\tau weights and differences where Vψ​(s)−Qϕ​(s,a)<0V_{\psi}(s)-Q_{\phi}(s,a)<0 get (τ−1)(\tau-1) weights. When τ>0.5\tau>0.5 this incentivizes predicting above the mean. The closer τ\tau is to 11 the more the value network is incentivized to over-shoot the Q-values.

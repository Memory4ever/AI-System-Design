V-C Model-level Stability
We analyze the parameter-space geometry induced by poisoning, which moves the model
from the pretrained initialization 𝚯0\bm{\Theta}_{0} to a poisoned solution 𝚯∗\bm{\Theta}_{\ast}.
The poisoning objective is augmented with two regularizers:
(i) an ℒEWC\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{EWC}} penalty centered at 𝚯0\bm{\Theta}_{0} (which bounds the displacement from 𝚯0\bm{\Theta}_{0} [61]),

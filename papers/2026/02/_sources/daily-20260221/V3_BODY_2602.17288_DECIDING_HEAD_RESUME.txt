Abstract
While frontier large language models demonstrate strong reasoning and
mathematical capabilities, the practical process of training
domain-specialized scientific language models from raw sources
remains under-documented. In this work, we present a detailed case
study of training a 1.36B-parameter scientific language model
directly from raw arXiv LaTeX sources spanning mathematics,
computer science, and theoretical physics.

4.1 Experimental Setup
Datasets.
We evaluated our approach on 2 representative benchmarks that cover event causal reasoning and video hallucination evaluation.
(1) Rextime [4] is an event causal relation-based Grounded VQA benchmark. It is designed for complex event reasoning and question answering across time, requiring the model to answer the reasoning question with consistent temporal localization. It contains 9695/921/2143 samples in train/val/test sets.
(2) VidHalluc [32] is a benchmark specifically for evaluating video hallucination for MLLM. It consists of 5002 videos with 9295 questions across 3 three critical dimensions for video hallucination evaluation, including Action Hallucination (ACH), Temporal Sequence Hallucination (TSH), and Scene Transition Hallucination (STH).

Implementation Details.
We used QwenVL-2.5-7B [2] as our baseline due to its strong video understanding ability and open-source availability. We also use this baseline for EVSG generation, including generating the multi-grained dense captions and building the EVSG process. The event number limit for dense caption is set to (5, 10, 15).

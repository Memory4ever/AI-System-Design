L233@P2: Approaches

L235@P2: LLMs memorize and leak personal data, how people perceive and

L614@P9: enforced by the instruction hierarchy. Given that only service providers have access to define
L615@P9: tools, this makes REIN a promising approach for safely and effectively steering the behavior of
L616@P9: fixed task agents.
L617@P9: 5 CONCLUSION
L618@P9: In this work, we addressed the practical challenge of conversational error recovery under highly
L619@P9: constrained conditions, where neither model parameters nor system prompts can be modified. Our
L620@P9: proposed test-time intervention approach, REIN, achieves substantial performance improvement by
L621@P9: diagnosing potential errors within the dialogue context and subsequently executing targeted recovery strategies. We further demonstrated that REIN can dynamically accommodate unobserved yet
L622@P9: relevant error types, enhancing its practicality for real-world deployment. Importantly, REIN operates effectively when appropriate recovery tools are jointly defined, making it a safer solution compared to external prompt injection methodologies. Beyond error recovery, the principles underlying
L623@P9: REIN could inform the development of adaptive, self-monitoring conversational agents capable of
L624@P9: autonomously detecting and mitigating performance degradation across evolving task domains without requiring retraining or prompt modification. We further discuss limitations and future directions
L625@P9: in §A.

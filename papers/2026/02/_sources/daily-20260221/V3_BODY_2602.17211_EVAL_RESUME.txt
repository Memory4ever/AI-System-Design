We consider data X∼pX\sim p distributed according to an unbalanced bimodal density p⁡(x)=𝒵−1​e−45​(x4−5​x2−x/2)p(x)=\mathcal{Z}^{-1}e^{-\tfrac{4}{5}(x^{4}-5x^{2}-x/2)} for x∈ℝx\in\mathbb{R}, and the four-dimensional monomial map ϕ⁡(x)=(x,x2,x3,x4)\phi(x)=(x,x^{2},x^{3},x^{4}), whose moments are 𝔼⁡[ϕ⁡(X)]≈(0.8,2.4,2.2,6.4)\mathbb{E}[\phi(X)]\approx(0.8,2.4,2.2,6.4). (Note that ItI_{t} for t∈(0,1)t\in(0,1) is not distributed according to the maximum entropy distribution with moments mtm_{t}.)
The log-density log⁡p∗​(x)\log p_{*}(x) (dotted line) in Figure 2(a) exhibits two modes, reflecting a non-convex Gibbs energy. For small σ\sigma, the density p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} concentrates in two separate modes. Although these modes do not have the correct shape (they are too peaked, reflecting the lack of entropy of p1σp_{1}^{\sigma}), their relative weight is correct. As σ\sigma increases, the added noise allows particles to spread correctly inside the modes, and p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} progressively converges towards p∗p_{*}, with near-superposition at σ2=5\sigma^{2}=5.

5.1.2 Slower Convergence
In the previous example, p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} converges towards p∗p_{*} with negligible error for σ2≥2\sigma^{2}\geq 2. We now show that the convergence constant CC in Conjecture 4.1 depends critically on the choice of moment functions ϕ\phi.
When pp is known, a seemingly natural choice is ϕ⁡(x)=(x2,log⁡p⁡(x))\phi(x)=(x^{2},\log p(x)), since this suffices to represent both the data density pp and the initial Gaussian density p0p_{0}, yielding p∗​(x)=p​(x)p_{*}(x)=p(x). For the bimodal density p⁡(x)=𝒵−1​e−45​(x4−5​x2−x/2)p(x)=\mathcal{Z}^{-1}e^{-\tfrac{4}{5}(x^{4}-5x^{2}-x/2)} with this ϕ\phi, Figures 2(e) and (f) confirm that DKL(p1σ∥p∗)D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_{1}^{\sigma}\|p_{*}) and H⁡(p∗)−H∗σH(p_{*})-H_{*}^{\sigma} both decay as C​σ−2C\sigma^{-2} for σ2≥50\sigma^{2}\geq 50, validating Conjectures 4.1 and 4.4. However, the constant CC is much larger than in the previous example: small errors require σ2≥500\sigma^{2}\geq 500, or approximately 10210^{2} times more integration steps.
Figure 2(d) shows the densities p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} for several values of σ\sigma. Although p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} is bimodal for σ2≤1\sigma^{2}\leq 1, the relative weights of the two modes are off by one order of magnitude. This occurs because ϕ\phi is not expressive enough to displace mass at early times tt of the MGD, before ptσp_{t}^{\sigma} becomes multimodal. For larger values of σ2\sigma^{2} (above 10210^{2}), MGD becomes analogous to a Langevin dynamic, recovering the correct relative weights through random switching of particles between modes.

5.1.3 Non-smooth ϕ\phi

We verify here that MGD correctly samples maximum entropy distributions defined by non-smooth ϕ\phi, and that Conjectures 4.1 and 4.4 hold.

We provide a theoretical justification for the volume expansion induced by our proposed GASS, as stated in Proposition 4.1.

The proof proceeds by establishing an explicit relationship between the geometric hypervolume of a point set and the Gram determinant of their inner product matrix. The crucial observation is that independent and orthogonal perturbations through expansion parameter rkr_{k} induce positive-definite corrections to the Gram determinant, which increases the hypervolume of the original point set.

Part 2.1: Commutativity of projection with perturbations
We first establish that perturbations applied to projected coordinates are mathematically equivalent to perturbations in the original space followed by projection.

For any vectors 𝐱\mathbf{x}, 𝐲\mathbf{y} and perturbations δ​𝐱\delta\mathbf{x}, δ​𝐲\delta\mathbf{y}, we have:

This ensures that when we perturb the projected coordinates along arbitrary 𝐮i\mathbf{u}_{i} and 𝐮j\mathbf{u}_{j}, the resulting changes to edge vectors in the projected space directly reflect the perturbations applied.

4.1 Convergence towards the Maximum Entropy Distribution
A central claim of this paper is that, as σ→∞\sigma\to\infty, the distribution p1σp_{1}^{\sigma} of the MGD output converges to the maximum entropy distribution p∗p_{*}. Next, we provide heuristic support for this claim via a formal Taylor expansion, then state it precisely as Conjecture 4.1. The conjecture is verified numerically in Section 5.
The time evolution of the PDF ptσp_{t}^{\sigma} of the solution of the MGD SDE (13) is governed by the Fokker-Planck equation:

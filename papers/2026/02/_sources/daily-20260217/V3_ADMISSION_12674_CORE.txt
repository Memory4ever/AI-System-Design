B49: 3.1 Original Reward Modeling
B50: The objective of experiential learning for knowledge distillation is to enable the student model π θ \pi_{\theta} to learn in the original environment in which the teacher model π \pi has learned. In other words, given the reward function R π R_{\pi} of the teacher’s original environment, the objective of experiential learning is to encourage the student model to maximize the expected reward, as shown in Equation 9 .
B51: max θ 𝔼 𝐱 ∼ 𝒟 , 𝐲 ∼ π θ ( ⋅ | 𝐱 ) [ R π ( 𝐱 , 𝐲 ) ] \max_{\theta}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x}\sim\mathcal{D},\mathbf{y}\sim\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|\mathbf{x})}[R_{\pi}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})] (9)
B52: However, the original reward function R π R_{\pi} is generally unknown. To estimate R π R_{\pi} , we assume that the original reward function follows a posterior distribution R π ∼ p ⁡ ( R | π ) R_{\pi}\sim p(R|\pi) and adopt a parameterized distribution q ϕ ​ ( R ) q_{\phi}(R) to approximate the posterior. The intuitive training objective of learning the original reward function is to minimize the KL divergence of q ϕ ​ ( R ) q_{\phi}(R) from p ⁡ ( R | π ) p(R|\pi) , as shown in Equation 10 . The equivalent objective is to maximize the ELBO of the KL divergence, as shown in Equation 11 .

B65: 3.2 Sequence-Level 𝒳 \mathcal{X} -KD
B66: Regarding the Boltzmann policy B θ B_{\theta} as the student policy, the ORM objective (Equation 13 ) can be reformulated as the combination of a knowledge distillation objective and an experiential objective:
B67: ℒ orm ​ ( ϕ , θ ) = ℒ seq ​ ( θ ) + ℒ ex ​ ( ϕ , θ ) \displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\text{orm}}(\phi,\theta)=\mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}}(\theta)+\mathcal{L}_{\text{ex}}(\phi,\theta) (17)
B68: Here, ℒ seq \mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}} is the sequence-level knowledge distillation (KD) objective ( Kim and Rush, 2016 ) which distills the knowledge in the teacher policy π \pi into the student policy B θ B_{\theta} :
B69: ℒ seq ​ ( θ ) \displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}}(\theta) = − ∑ 𝐱 ∈ 𝒟 𝔼 𝐲 ∼ π ( ⋅ | 𝐱 ) [ log B θ ( 𝐲 | 𝐱 ) ] \displaystyle=-\sum_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{D}}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y}\sim\pi(\cdot|\mathbf{x})}\left[\log B_{\theta}(\mathbf{y}|\mathbf{x})\right] (18)
B70: = − ∑ 𝐱 ∈ 𝒟 𝔼 𝐲 ∼ π ( ⋅ | 𝐱 ) [ ∑ t = 1 | 𝐲 | log B θ ( y t + 1 | 𝐱 , 𝐲 1 : t ) ] \displaystyle=-\sum_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{D}}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y}\sim\pi(\cdot|\mathbf{x})}\left[\sum_{t=1}^{|\mathbf{y}|}\log B_{\theta}(y_{t+1}|\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}_{1:t})\right]
B71: ℒ ex \mathcal{L}_{\text{ex}} is the experiential objective ensuring the reward distribution to satisfy the prior distribution p ⁡ ( R ) p(R) and ensuring the student policy B θ B_{\theta} to satisfy the TD-error constraint under the reward distribution:
B72: ℒ ex ( ϕ , θ ) = ∑ 𝐱 ∈ 𝒟 𝔼 𝐲 ∼ π ( ⋅ | 𝐱 ) [ ℱ ex ( 𝐱 , 𝐲 ; ϕ , θ ) ] \mathcal{L}_{\text{ex}}(\phi,\theta)=\sum_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{D}}\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y}\sim\pi(\cdot|\mathbf{x})}\left[\mathcal{F}_{\text{ex}}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y};\phi,\theta)\right] (19)
B73: where ℱ ex \mathcal{F}_{\text{ex}} is the sample-wise experiential objective:
B74: ℱ ex ​ ( 𝐱 , 𝐲 , ϕ , θ ) = ∑ t = 1 | 𝐲 | [ D KL [ q ϕ ( ⋅ | 𝐱 , 𝐲 1 : t + 1 ) | | p ( ⋅ ) ] − λ log q ϕ ( δ t ( θ ) | 𝐱 , 𝐲 1 : t + 1 ) ] \mathcal{F}_{\text{ex}}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y};\phi,\theta)=\sum_{t=1}^{|\mathbf{y}|}\left[\begin{aligned} D_{\text{KL}}[q_{\phi}(\cdot|\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}_{1:t+1})||p(\cdot)]\\ -\lambda\log q_{\phi}(\delta_{t}(\theta)|\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}_{1:t+1})\end{aligned}\right] (20)
B75: Here, the hyperparameter λ \lambda is referred to as the experiential weight since it directly controls the degree of TD-error constraint.

By minimizing ℒ orm \mathcal{L}_{\text{orm}} , the student policy B θ B_{\theta} performs sequence-level knowledge distillation from the teacher policy π \pi through the minimization of ℒ seq \mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}} , while staying consistent with the reward function of the teacher’s original learning environment through the minimization of ℒ ex \mathcal{L}_{\text{ex}} , thus achieving experiential learning.

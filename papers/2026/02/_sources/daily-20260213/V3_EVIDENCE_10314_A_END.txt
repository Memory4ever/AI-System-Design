L408: We start by showing the equality
L409: 
L410:  | $$\mathbb{P}(x_{0}\mid x_{t})\propto\mathbf{1}\{x_{0}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}=x_{t}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}\}\,p_{\mathrm{data}}(x_{0}).$$  |
L411: 
L412: Fix $t\in(0,1)$ and a masked context $x_{t}\in(\mathcal{V}\cup\{\mathbf{m}\})^{L}$. Write $\mathbf{um}(x_{t})\subseteq[L]$ for the unmasked coordinates and $\mathbf{msk}(x_{t}):=\{i:x_{t}^{i}=\mathbf{m}\}$. By assumption,
L413:  | $$p(x_{t}\mid x_{0},t)\ \propto\ \alpha_{t}(x_{t})\,\mathbf{1}\{x_{0}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}=x_{t}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}\}.$$  |  | (6)
L414: 
L415: Then Bayes’ rule gives, for any $x_{0}$,
L416: 
L417:  | $$\mathbb{P}(x_{0}\mid x_{t},t)\ \propto\ p(x_{t}\mid x_{0},t)\,p_{\mathrm{data}}(x_{0})\ \propto\ \alpha_{t}(x_{t})\,\mathbf{1}\{x_{0}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}=x_{t}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}\}\,p_{\mathrm{data}}(x_{0}),$$  |
L418: The factor $\alpha_{t}(x_{t})$ does not depend on $x_{0}$ and cancels after normalization. Furthermore, the dependency on $t$ also disappears, yielding the data conditional form
L419: 
L420:  | $$\mathbb{P}(x_{0}\mid x_{t})=\mathbb{P}(x_{0}\mid x_{t},t)\propto\mathbf{1}\{x_{0}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}=x_{t}^{\mathbf{um}(x_{t})}\}\,p_{\mathrm{data}}(x_{0}).$$  |  | (7)
L421: 
L422: In particular, the right-hand side depends on $x_{t}$ only through its revealed coordinates, and thus does not depend on $\alpha_{t}$.
L423: Now let $({\mathbf{x}}_{0},t,{\mathbf{x}}_{t})\sim p_{f}$. Fix a coordinate $i\in[L]$ and define the random variables
L424: 
L425:  | $$X:={\mathbf{x}}_{t},\qquad Y:={\mathbf{x}}_{0}^{i}\in\mathcal{V}.$$  |
L426: 
L427: The contribution of coordinate $i$ to the loss is
L428: 
L429:  | $$\mathcal{L}_{i}(\theta):=\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{1}{t}\,\mathbf{1}\{X^{i}=\mathbf{m}\}\,\big(-\log f_{\theta}^{i}(Y\mid X)\big)\Big].$$  |
L430: Conditioning on $(X,t)$ and using (cite119†7 ) (which implies $\mathbb{P}(Y=\cdot\mid X,t)=\mathbb{P}(Y=\cdot\mid X)$), we can write
L431: 
L432:  | $\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{i}(\theta)$  | $\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{1}{t}\,\mathbf{1}\{X^{i}=\mathbf{m}\}\,\mathbb{E}\big[-\log f_{\theta}^{i}(Y\mid X)\mid X,t\big]\Big]$  |
L433:  |  | $\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\Big[w_{i}(X)\,\mathbb{E}\big[-\log f_{\theta}^{i}(Y\mid X)\mid X\big]\Big],$  |
L434: where $w_{i}(X):=\frac{1}{t}\,\mathbf{1}\{X^{i}=\mathbf{m}\}\geq 0$ (note that we are assuming $t>0$). Thus, by Lemma cite120†4 , any minimizer $f_{\theta}^{*}$ equals the true posterior $\mathbb{P}(x_{0}\mid x_{t})$, which finishes the proof. ∎

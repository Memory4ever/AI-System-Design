# arXiv exact holiday date evidence

Primary: https://blog.arxiv.org/2025/11/21/temporary-changes-to-announcement-schedule-due-to-end-of-year-holidays-2025/
Published by arXiv, 2025-11-21. Read whole official page; decisive lines 17, 21, 29, 32 in web extraction.

Official schedule: Dec 30, 2025 has no announcement. Accepted submissions received at/after 14:00 ET Dec 29 and before 14:00 ET Dec 31 are deferred to 20:00 ET Dec 31. Jan 1, 2026 likewise has no announcement; next deferred new-submission batch is 20:00 ET Jan 4. New submissions' public availability is deferred along with mailings. Existing papers remain accessible. These rules concern new submissions, not a proof that revisions or author mirrors cannot change.

ET is EST (UTC-05) in this winter interval. Our start is Dec 30 20:00 ET = 2025-12-31T09:00+08; our excluded end is Dec 31 20:00 ET = 2026-01-01T09:00+08. Thus no scheduled first-announcement batch falls inside this day. This is an actual date-specific official announcement, not a guessed weekday schedule.

Availability policy: https://info.arxiv.org/help/availability.html . Identifiers are assigned at first announcement and cannot be backdated. Submitted is not public. DOI registration is not public time; together with identifier policy it can cap an arXiv identifier's already-assigned interval, not date an earlier author mirror. DataCite Updated and Available-month fields are not sufficient day gates.

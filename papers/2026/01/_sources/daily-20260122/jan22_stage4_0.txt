L12: # Attention Authors: Temporary change to announcement schedule due to MLK Jr. Holiday
L13: 
L14: By Kat Boboris·January 14^{th} 2026
L15: 
L16: This coming Monday, January 19, 2026, arXiv staff will be observing Martin Luther King Jr. Day. This holiday will temporarily affect arXiv’s mailings, help desk, and announcement schedule.
L17: 
L18: Submissions to arXiv are typically made public on cite4†arXiv.org†arxiv.org and cite5†announced by email†arxiv.org on a cite6†regular schedule†arxiv.org .
L19: As our team celebrates MLK Day 2026, announcements will be slightly deferred. The announcement of new submissions is the only change related to this holiday. arXiv servers will otherwise remain in operation, existing papers will be available to browse, and arXiv will continue to accept submissions.
L20: 
L21: As you plan your submissions to arXiv this week, please be aware of the following changes:
L22: Monday, January 19, 2026: No announcement or help desk support. Articles received and accepted at or after 14:00ET Friday, January 16, 2026, and before 14:00 ET Tuesday, January 20, 2026, will be announced on Tuesday, January 20, 2026.

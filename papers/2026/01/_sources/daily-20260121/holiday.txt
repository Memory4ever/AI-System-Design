L13: 
L14: By Kat Boboris·January 14^{th} 2026
L15: 
L16: This coming Monday, January 19, 2026, arXiv staff will be observing Martin Luther King Jr. Day. This holiday will temporarily affect arXiv’s mailings, help desk, and announcement schedule.

L19: As our team celebrates MLK Day 2026, announcements will be slightly deferred.

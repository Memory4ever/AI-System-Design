L137:   * cite70†Kimi K3 : Kimi K3 is a 2.8T-parameter model built on Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) and Attention Residuals (AttnRes), with native vision capabilities and a 1-million-token context window. L138:   * cite71†Attention Residuals : a drop-in replacement for residual connections with consistent scaling gains.

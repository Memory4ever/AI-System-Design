3.3 Proof of Theorem 2.1
Before proof the main theorem, we first present some useful lemmas.
Lemma 3.1.
Assume that Assumption 2.1-2.5 hold and let the sequence \{\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\} be generated via iterations of PnP-PGD with the mismatched MMSE denoiser and \lambda L_{f}<1. Then for the objective function F_{\lambda,\sigma} defined above, we have
(17)
F_{\lambda,\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})\leq F_{\lambda,\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1}-\frac{1-L_{f}}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}.
Proof.
Under Assumption 2.5, denoiser \widehat{\mathsf{D}}_{\sigma} is an approximation of target MMSE denoiser, at k+1-th iteration, suppose that \boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}=\widehat{\mathsf{D}}_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{z}_{k+1}), we have
\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})+\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}\leq\min_{\boldsymbol{u}}\left(\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{u})+\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{u}-\boldsymbol{z}_{k+1}\|^{2}\right)+\epsilon_{k+1},
Introducing the majorization-minimization (MM) function
Q(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{y})=\lambda f(\boldsymbol{y})+\lambda\left<\boldsymbol{x}-\boldsymbol{y},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{y})\right>+\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}-\boldsymbol{y}\|^{2}+\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}),
satisfies
Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\leq\min_{\boldsymbol{x}}Q(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1},
since
\min_{\boldsymbol{x}}Q(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\leq Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})=F_{\lambda,\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k}),
we have
Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\leq Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1}.
Then, note that Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\leq Q(\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1} implies that
(18)
\displaystyle\lambda f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\lambda\left<\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\right>+\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}+\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})
\displaystyle\leq\lambda f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1}
(19)
\displaystyle\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})\leq\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})-\lambda\left<\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\right>
\displaystyle-\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}+\epsilon_{k+1}
using the descent lemma
f(\boldsymbol{x})\leq f(\boldsymbol{y})+\left<\boldsymbol{x}-\boldsymbol{y},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{y})\right>+\frac{1}{2t}\|\boldsymbol{x}-\boldsymbol{y}\|^{2}
with t=\frac{1}{L_{f}} and relation (19) we get
\displaystyle F_{\lambda,\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})
\displaystyle=\lambda f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})+\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1})
\displaystyle\leq\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})-\lambda\left<\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\right>-\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}+\epsilon_{k+1}+\lambda f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+
\displaystyle\qquad\lambda\left<\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k},\nabla f(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})\right>+\frac{L_{f}}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2}
\displaystyle=F_{\lambda,\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x}_{k})+\epsilon_{k+1}-\frac{1-L_{f}}{2}\|\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_{k}\|^{2},
this complete the proof.
∎

(20)
H(\boldsymbol{u})=\mathop{\arg\min}_{\boldsymbol{u}}\frac{1}{2}\|\boldsymbol{u}-\boldsymbol{z}\|^{2}+\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{u}),
we have
(21)
\begin{cases}\nabla\phi_{\sigma}(\hat{\boldsymbol{x}})=\boldsymbol{z}-\hat{\boldsymbol{x}}-\boldsymbol{\delta}\\
\|\boldsymbol{\delta}\|\leq\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon}{1-L}}.\end{cases}

\nabla^{2}H(\boldsymbol{x})=I+\nabla^{2}\phi_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{x})=\left[J_{D_{\sigma}(\boldsymbol{y})}\right]^{-1}.

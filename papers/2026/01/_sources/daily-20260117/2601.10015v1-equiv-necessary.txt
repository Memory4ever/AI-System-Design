B.2. Proof of Proposition 1
Proposition 1 shows that cross-client KD can be decoupled as teacher exit aggregation at server and local KD at clients.
We prove Proposition 1 below.
Proof.
Given input sample x, the KL divergence of P(x) and Q(x) is defined as:
(20)
D_{KL}(P||Q)=P(x)\log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}
Taking Eq. (20) into Eq. (4), there is:
(21)
\displaystyle\theta^{*}
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\theta_{im}(x)\log\frac{\theta_{im}(x)}{\theta(x)}
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\theta_{im}(x)(\log\theta_{im}(x)-\log\theta(x))
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}-\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\theta_{im}(x)\log\theta(x)
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}-\log\theta(x)\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\theta_{im}(x)
The model \theta_{im} can be further expressed as \theta_{im}=(\phi,h_{im}).
The global backbone \phi is shared by all clients.
The linear classifier h_{im} is kept locally.
Let the model with the aggregated classifier be \bar{\theta}=(\phi,\bar{h}).
The averaged classifier matrix is \bar{h}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}h_{im}.
Let the output of block m of backbone \phi be \phi(x), we have:
(22)
\displaystyle\bar{\theta}(x)=\bar{h}(\phi(x))=\phi(x)^{\top}\bar{h}=\phi(x)^{\top}\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}h_{im}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\phi(x)^{\top}h_{im}
Since the backbone \phi is shared across all clients, the output of exit \theta_{im} can be represented by:
(23)
\displaystyle\theta_{im}(x)
\displaystyle=h_{im}(\phi(x))
\displaystyle=\phi(x)^{\top}h_{im}
Taking Eq. (23) into Eq. (22), there is:
(24)
\bar{\theta}(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}k_{i}\theta_{im}(x)
Taking Eq. (24) into Eq. (21), we have:
(25)
\displaystyle\theta^{*}
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}-\bar{\theta}(x)\log\theta(x)
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}\bar{\theta}(x)(\log\bar{\theta}(x)-\log\theta(x))
\displaystyle=\arg\min_{\theta}D_{KL}(\bar{\theta}||\theta)
Combining Eq. (4) and Eq. (25), we complete the proof.
∎

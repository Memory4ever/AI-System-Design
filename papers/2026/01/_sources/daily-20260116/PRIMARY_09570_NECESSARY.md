III-B Stalling Index (SI): Interaction Health Telemetry III-B 1 Design Philosophy: Observable Failure Signatures Productive information-gathering dialogues exhibit two observable properties: (1) queries yield responses with measurable information content, and (2) successive queries explore semantically distinct aspects. When these degrade (i.e., repeated category probing yields semantically similar responses with minimal knowledge gain) the interaction has entered an unproductive state we term conversational stalling . SI monitors this pattern through observable turn-level features. When a category is repeatedly queried within a short window with high semantic similarity between exchanges and minimal knowledge gain, SI rises. This design prioritizes actionable signals over causal attribution, recognizing that field systems may lack reliable methods for diagnosing whether unprod

The verifier first locates a dedicated final-answer segment using an explicit marker (e.g., Final Answer: ). The extracted string is then lightly normalized to remove non-semantic variation such as whitespace and punctuation differences, and to strip common answer-introduction phrases (e.g., “Answer is …”).

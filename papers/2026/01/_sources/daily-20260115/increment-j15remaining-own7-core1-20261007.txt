L151: ### 3.2 On-policy Distillation for Frontier Policy Exploitation
L152: 
L153: While the exploration stage identifies frontier policies, REINFORCE-style updates provide only a sparse $O(1)$ supervision signal per rollout sequence (uniform $\hat{A}$ across all tokens). L156: #### On-Policy Distillation Mode Fusing
L157: 
L158: Common intuition trains distillation with an offline dataset with query and responses sampled from the teacher model and train the student in an SFT manner.

L243: T^{3} evaluates causal judgment in large language models across Pearl’s three levels (Association, Intervention, Counterfactuals) using natural-language vignettes that embed formal causal traps. Unlike benchmarks that report only aggregate accuracy, T^{3} decomposes performance into Utility (sensitivity) and Safety (specificity), and separately evaluates Wise Refusal on genuinely underdetermined cases (illustrated in Figure cite158†1 ).

L787: ### C.3 Wise Refusal Guidelines
L788: 
L789: For AMBIGUOUS cases, annotators were instructed to mark an item as underdetermined only if the missing information is critical to the causal logic (e.g., ”Did Alice press the button before or after the light turned on?”), rather than trivial background details.

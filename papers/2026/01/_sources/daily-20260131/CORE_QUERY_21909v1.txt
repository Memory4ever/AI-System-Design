3.2.1 Identifying Computed Intermediate Results
During RL training, given a problem qq, the model generates a reasoning trajectory y=(y1,y2,…,yT)y=(y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{T}), where each yt∈𝒱y_{t}\in\mathcal{V} is a token from the vocabulary and TT is the sequence length. Unlike the meta-thought trajectories τmeta\tau_{\text{meta}} from CoMT training which describe abstract reasoning steps, these trajectories yy contain complete solutions with specific numerical computations leading to a final answer.
To measure confidence in intermediate reasoning steps, we focus on the numerical tokens within the generated trajectory, as these intermediate computations are where overconfident errors originate and cascade through subsequent steps. Let 𝒩q\mathcal{N}_{q} denote the set of numbers appearing in the problem statement qq. For each numerical token yty_{t} in the trajectory, we classify it into one of two categories: Extracted numbers are numerical tokens that directly appear in the problem, i.e., yt∈𝒩qy_{t}\in\mathcal{N}_{q}; Computed numbers are numerical tokens produced through intermediate calculations, i.e., yt∉𝒩qy_{t}\notin\mathcal{N}_{q}.
We focus specifically on computed numbers, as these represent the model’s intermediate computational results. Let 𝒞\mathcal{C} denote the set of indices corresponding to computed number tokens: 𝒞={t:yt​ is numeric and ​yt∉𝒩q}\mathcal{C}=\{t:y_{t}\text{ is numeric and }y_{t}\notin\mathcal{N}_{q}\}.

4.2.3 Ablation Study
To isolate the contribution of CCRL, we conduct an ablation study comparing standard RL (using only outcome-based rewards) with our method on top of CoMT-tuned models. Figure 2 presents the results across four base models on GSM8K and SVAMP benchmarks. CCRL consistently improves upon standard RL across all settings. CCRL outperforms standard outcome-based RL with improvements ranging from +0.2% to +2.5% across all models and datasets.The uniformly positive gains of +0.65 points on average demonstrate that confidence-aware feedback addresses a distinct aspect of reasoning optimization complementary to meta-knowledge acquisition: while CoMT provides larger gains through abstract reasoning strategies (+3.91 points average), CCRL contributes additional improvements by encouraging appropriate confidence calibration during problem-specific execution.

A.1 Softmax bottleneck and singular entropy
We state the formal lemma and define the diagnostic used in §3.
The saturation problem follows Godey et al. (2024). Let rr denote the dimension of the final-layer representation (equivalently, an upper bound on the rank of the output head). The final linear layer maps an rr-dimensional representation to a distribution over a vocabulary of size VV, which can be viewed as learning a low-rank approximation Wr∈ℝV×rW_{r}\in\mathbb{R}^{V\times r} of a theoretically optimal, high-rank matrix W∗W^{*}.

5.3 Post-unlearning Recovery
Relearning via fine-tuning.
Figure 9(a) shows results when further fine-tuning unlearned models on:
i) mixed retain and unrelated data,
ii) retain-only, and
iii) unrelated-only.
All methods exhibit reduced F.D., indicating partial recovery, while our approach remains the most robust across settings.
Quantization.
Compressing models to int4 amplifies residual memorization and can revive information; see Figure 9(b)
Most baselines experience a pronounced drop in F.D., whereas ours maintains high F.D. under quantization.

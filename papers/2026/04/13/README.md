#### Dynamic sparsity in tree-structured feed-forward layers at scale

<!-- claim:SF-2026-ARXIV-2604-08565:start -->

- **Old path / changed constraint:** As models scale to billions of parameters, the cost of dense FF blocks has become a primary bottleneck, motivating extensive research into sparse alternatives.
- **Mechanism / ownership:** Detailed results are reported in the Appendix D In Appendix C , we present additional results for 2.7B and 6.7B parameter OPT models showing promising results for the d = 4 d=4 FFF variant.
- **Evaluation contract:** We show that simple architectural choices can modulate this behavior and recover balanced trees without auxiliary losses.

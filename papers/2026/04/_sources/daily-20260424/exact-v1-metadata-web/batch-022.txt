L15: # Title:Ion Channel Dynamics in Temperature-Dependent Weibel Instability Saturation
L16: 
L17: Authors:cite9†Vivek Shrivastav , cite10†Mani K Chettri , cite11†Hemam D Singh , cite12†Britan Singh , cite13†Rupak Mukherjee1 L18: 
L19: View a PDF of the paper titled Ion Channel Dynamics in Temperature-Dependent Weibel Instability Saturation, by Vivek Shrivastav and 4 other authors
L20: 
L21: cite14†View PDF cite15†HTML (experimental) L22: > Abstract:We present 1X2V continuum Vlasov-Maxwell simulations of interpenetrating plasma beams with mobile ions. While the early-time evolution is similar to the stationary-ion case, the late-time dynamics are dominated by the ion-Weibel instability. As ion channels merge, the magnetic energy increases and the magnetic structures extend further along the beam direction. Electrons rapidly reach thermal equilibrium, whereas ions retain distinct bulk velocities for much longer and thermalize more slowly.
L23: These results are relevant to collisionless shock formation in astrophysical compact objects and laser-plasma experiments. Wind/SWE observations place all four simulated cases in the firehose/Weibel-unstable region of the proton temperature anisotropy diagram, and MMS1 observations of a quasi-perpendicular bow shock ($\theta_{Bn}\approx83^\circ$, $M_A\approx27$) show a similar electron-ion thermalization disparity.

L9: # Title:A theory of generalized Lamé curves
L10: 
L11: Authors:cite6†You-Cheng Chou , cite7†Chin-Lung Wang , cite8†Po-Sheng Wu L12: 
L13: View a PDF of the paper titled A theory of generalized Lam\'e curves, by You-Cheng Chou and 2 other authors
L14: 
L15: cite9†View PDF cite10†HTML (experimental) L16: > Abstract:We study the generalized Lamé equation on an elliptic curve $E$ with multiple singularities. By restricting to the locus admitting solutions with quasi-periodic properties, we construct two curves:
L17: > (i) The generalized Lam'e curve: with $n:=\sum\nolimits_{i=1}^r n_i\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}$, we construct $\mathcal{Y}_{\mathbf n}(\mathbf p;\tau)$, which lies in an affine bundle over $Sym^n E$ and parametrizes generalized Hermite--Halphen ansatz solutions.
L18: > (ii) The log-free curve: each $n_i\in\frac12\mathbb N$ gives a polynomial equation in the accessory parameters. This leads to a non-complete intersection variety $V_{\mathbf{n}}(\mathbf{p};\tau)$ when all $n_i\in\frac12\mathbb N$. We prove that it is a reduced curve.
L19: > We analysis the GLC as an algebraic family over the pole configuration space $\mathbf{p}$. We study the shifted addition map \[ \sigma: Sym^n E\longrightarrow E, \] establishing a generically finite, degree formula. The geometry of boundary degenerations under pole collisions perfectly mirrors the tensor algebra of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})$-modules within the BGG category $\mathcal{O}$.
L20: > We generalize pre-modular forms to a framework of twisted isomonodromic deformations. We construct $(\mathbf{n}, \mathbf{p})$-deformed pre-modular forms parameterized by pseudo-monodromy data $(t,s)$, whose vanishing governs these deformations and factorizes along boundary strata. Iterating these deformations through the boundary allows any arbitrary configuration to be continuously deformed down to the classical Lamé equation.
L21: > Finally, we prove the Treibich conjecture stated for $r=2$ extra symmetric pairs, as well as its generalizations for $r \leq 4$.

L9: # Title:Geometric Decoupling and the Color--Speed Mechanism in Multispeed Stochastic Klein--Gordon Systems
L10: 
L11: Authors:cite6†Guangqian Zhao L12: 
L13: View a PDF of the paper titled Geometric Decoupling and the Color--Speed Mechanism in Multispeed Stochastic Klein--Gordon Systems, by Guangqian Zhao
L14: 
L15: cite7†View PDF cite8†HTML (experimental) L16: > Abstract:We study a two-component fractional stochastic Klein--Gordon system on $\mathbb{T}^3$ driven by independent space-time white noises. Our main result is the identification of a new color-speed separation principle for the mixed paracontrolled random operators in the multispeed regime. This principle is created by the interaction between probabilistic cancellation, multispeed dispersive phase separation, and the nonlinear random-operator structure generated by the paracontrolled expansion.
L17: At the level of the nonlinear expansion, it reorganizes the singular contraction geometry of the mixed random operators: cross-color contraction channels vanish by independence, while the surviving same-color contractions are forced into speed-separated oscillatory interactions and therefore become finite Volterra-type deterministic multipliers.
L18: In this way, the multispeed structure does not merely alter the linear phase geometry; it changes the nonlinear singular interaction mechanism itself and regularizes the most singular contraction channels produced by the quadratic coupling.
L19: > We prove the almost sure pathwise convergence of the centered fluctuation operators and the convergence of the same-color deterministic contractions, with no singular random-operator centering required in the limiting equation. Our analysis identifies $\alpha = \frac{3}{4}$ as the intrinsic structural threshold of the mixed random-operator algebra. In addition, we construct the baseline stochastic lift and formulate a conditional deterministic closure for the full paracontrolled system.
L20: These results show that, in coupled singular dispersive SPDEs, multispeed propagation can act as a geometric regularizer precisely through its effect on the nonlinear random-operator structure.
L21: Comments:  | Identifies a geometric color--speed decoupling mechanism for mixed random operators in a multispeed fractional stochastic Klein--Gordon system: independence eliminates the cross-color channel, while speed separation regularizes the same-color channel, yielding no singular centering and the intrinsic operator threshold $α_c^{\mathrm{op}} = 3/4$

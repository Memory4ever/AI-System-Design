L15: cite7†View PDF cite8†HTML (experimental) L16: > Abstract:In this article, we study loop-induced Higgs decays into gauge bosons within the framework of Radiative Natural Supersymmetry. We reproduce the one-loop MSSM calculations for the Higgs partial decay widths into a Z boson-photon pair, two photons, and two gluons, providing the corresponding analytical expressions for the scattering amplitudes.
L17: We focus on the region of parameter space that maximizes the rare decay width of the process $h \to Z\gamma$, and analyze the correlated predictions for the remaining Higgs decay channels. In the selected region of parameter space, the $h \to Z\gamma$ decay width is enhanced, reaching a maximum value of $\simeq 7.5~\mathrm{keV}$, while remaining compatible with the current ATLAS measurement.
L18: At the same time, this region satisfies current Higgs constraints from the $h \to \gamma\gamma$ and $h \to gg$ channels. The diphoton mode remains close to the Standard Model expectation, with deviations at the level of $\lesssim 5\%$. The gluon channel exhibits a stronger sensitivity to the considered region of parameter space, leading to a moderate suppression of about $12\%$ in the corresponding partial width.

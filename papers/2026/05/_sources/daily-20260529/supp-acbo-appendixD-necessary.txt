D.1  Proof of Lemma  1  (Near-Miss Kernel Similarity) 

 

 
 Lemma 3   ( Token overlap implies kernel similarity ) .

and each contribution equals  ‖ ∇ θ z θ , t ‖ 2 ≥ 0 \|\nabla_{\theta}z_{\theta,t}\|^{2}\geq 0 .

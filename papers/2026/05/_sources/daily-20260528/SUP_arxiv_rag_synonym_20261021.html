While there are many reinforcement learning (RL) methods for training the L… ▽ More Large-scale search, recommendation, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems typically employ a two-stage architecture: an early-stage ranker (ESR) generates a candidate set, which is subsequently re-ranked by a late-stage ranker (LSR). While there are many reinforcement learning (RL) methods for training the LSR, end-to-end training of the ESR has proven challenging. In particular, naive application of "vanilla" policy gradient (V-PG) is not scalable for candidate-set sizes relevant for practical use due to exploding variance.

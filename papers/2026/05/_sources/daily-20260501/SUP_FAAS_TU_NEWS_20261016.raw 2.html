    Paper accepted at EdgeSys '26

    28/04/2026

    Our paper “FaaSMoE: A Serverless Framework for Multi-Tenant Mixture-of-Experts Serving” has been accepted at EdgeSys ’26.

    We introduce FaaSMoE, a new approach to serving Mixture-of-Experts models that eliminates one of their biggest inefficiencies: keeping all experts in memory at all times. By turning experts into serverless functions, FaaSMoE enables dynamic, on-demand execution across multiple tenants.

    This results in a dramatic reduction in resource usage—down to less than one third compared to conventional deployments—while preserving the flexibility and scalability needed for modern AI workloads (PDF available under Publications). 

    We’ve got the brains for the future.
    For the benefit of society.

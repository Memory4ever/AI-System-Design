L11: 
L12: Our research teams investigate the safety, inner workings, and societal impacts of AI models—so that artificial intelligence has a positive impact as it becomes increasingly capable.

L16: 
L17: The Alignment team works to understand the risks of AI models and develop ways to ensure that future ones remain helpful, honest, and harmless.
L18: 
L19: ### Economics
L20: 
L21: The Economics team studies how AI is reshaping the economy, including work, productivity, and economic opportunity.
L22: 
L23: ### Frontier Red Team
L24: 
L25: The Frontier Red Team analyzes the implications of frontier AI models for cybersecurity, biosecurity, and autonomous systems.
L26: ### Interpretability
L27: 
L28: The mission of the Interpretability team is to understand how large language models work internally, as a foundation for AI safety and positive outcomes.
L29: ### Societal Impacts
L30: 
L31: Working closely with the Anthropic Policy and Safeguards teams, Societal Impacts is a technical research team that explores how AI is used in the real world.

Table 5 details the total inference costs for each evaluation setting. All agent inference calls (using Claude Sonnet 4.5) were executed via the Anthropic API. While the multi-agent scaffold more than doubles the total inference cost compared to the single-agent baselines, the absolute financial cost per task remains negligible. We argue that this increase in compute expenditure is a highly favorable trade-off; by proactively resolving underspecification and significantly increasing the overall task resolve rate, the multi-agent setup ultimately saves substantial human developer time and effort that would otherwise be spent on debugging solutions that are misaligned with the original intent.

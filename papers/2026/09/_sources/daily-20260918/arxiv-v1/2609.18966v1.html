The Automaton Underneath:
The Additive Input Pathway Is a Parasitic Attractor
for State Tracking in Householder Linear RNNs

Gunner Levi Howe
gunnerlevihowe@gmail.com
(July 2026)
Abstract

Linear RNNs with input-dependent Householder-product transitions (DeltaNet/DeltaProduct-class) can provably represent hard state-tracking automata, yet trained models notoriously fail to length-generalize — a gap recent work names “expressivity vs. learnability” and explicitly attributes to optimization, without a causal account. We give one, in a pre-registered, within-architecture causal ablation: the same model, with one term deleted — the additive input injection bt=Wb​etb_{t}=W_{b}e_{t}. With btb_{t}, models fit length 32 perfectly and collapse out-of-distribution on parity, S4S_{4}, A5A_{5}, and the non-solvable S5S_{5} word problems (median position-512 accuracy 0.200.20 on S5S_{5}). Without btb_{t} — input acting only through the orthogonal transitions — the same architecture at the same width learns the exact automaton: median accuracy 1.001.00 at 16×16\times the training length, at every width admitted by a representation law we state and test: the minimal number of Householder factors per token equals the maximal reflection length of the task’s generators in the representation the task format pins (parity 11, S4S_{4} 33, A5A_{5} and S5S_{5} 44); below it, −b-b models cannot fit at all, and the contrast with DeltaProduct’s S4/A5S_{4}/A_{5}-at-nh=2n_{h}{=}2 (group-element classification, S​O​(3)SO(3) realization) shows the law is representation-relative rather than absolute. Two pre-registered discriminators locate the mechanism. (i) Initialized at a verified-exact solution with Wb=0W_{b}{=}0, Adam grows the additive path (probe ∥Wb​e∥\lVert W_{b}e\rVert 0→0.770\!\to\!0.77 on S5S_{5}) and pulls the model off the exact solution (0.230.23 at position 512) while −b-b controls stay at 1.001.00 — the additive route is an attractor, not merely a basin around random inits. (ii) Our pre-registered prediction that the shortcut’s in-domain fit lives on btb_{t} was wrong, and the registered kill criterion fired: zeroing WbW_{b} at inference leaves in-domain fit intact in all 49 fitting seeds — and at the law-minimal width it restores exact length generalization (parity 5/55/5, S5S_{5} 5/55/5, S4S_{4} 4/54/5, A5A_{5} 4/54/5 seeds). The additive pathway is parasitic: a norm-inflation channel that cross-entropy feeds, which first destabilizes and then conceals a correctly learned automaton. Boundaries are reported with the same discipline: above-law widths restore in only 11–2/52/5 seeds, and the cyclic mod-3 task drifts rather than locking even without btb_{t} — killing our own earlier “transposition anchor” hypothesis, since all-even-generator A5A_{5} locks exactly. All 202 runs, predictions, and kill criteria were pre-registered in-repository before any grid run; every number regenerates from run artifacts.

1 Introduction

State tracking — maintaining the running state of an automaton while reading a sequence — is the sharpest known separation between attention/parallel architectures and true recurrence [6, 2], and the leading sub-quadratic architectures (DeltaNet-class linear RNNs with input-dependent orthogonal or near-orthogonal transitions) were designed in part to close it [4, 10]. A striking 2025–2026 literature documents that they close it in principle but not in training: models that provably contain exact solutions fit the training length and collapse beyond it [9, 7, 1, 2]. Shakerinava et al. [9] name the gap — “the main bottleneck …is not expressivity but optimization” — demonstrate it survives near-solution initialization in diagonal SSMs, and call for a loss-landscape account. Cross-architecture evidence points at the input pathway: Terzić et al. [13] report that diagonal selective SSMs without the additive BB matrix length-generalize better on commutative automata; Ebrahimi and Memisevic [3] show pure bilinear (non-affine) RNNs length-generalize on parity and random permutation FSMs where affine variants fail; and injection-free orthogonal-transition architectures extrapolate spectacularly [12, 5]. But none of these is a causal test: each compares different architectures (or removes capacity along with the pathway, as in diagonal SSMs, which lose non-commutative expressivity with BB), none explains why gradient descent prefers the failing solution, and PD-SSM’s exact S5S_{5} extrapolation with an additive term [14] falsifies any architecture-universal reading.

This note supplies the missing experiment, pre-registered end-to-end. In a fixed DeltaProduct-style Householder linear RNN — where the orthogonal transitions alone are expressive enough for every task studied, so deleting the additive injection btb_{t} changes what SGD finds but not what the model can represent — we delete exactly one term and observe, across five seeds per cell:

  1. 1.

    The flip (§3): +b+b models fit length 32 and collapse (shortcut); −b-b models learn the exact automaton to length 512 (16×16\times), including the non-solvable S5S_{5}, at matched width, budget, and data.

  2. 2.

    A representation law with a necessity side (§4): the minimal nhn_{h} that length-generalizes equals the maximal generator reflection length rank⁡(I−P)\operatorname{rank}(I-P) in the representation pinned by the task format (parity 1, S4S_{4} 3, A5A_{5}/S5S_{5} 4); every below-law −b-b cell is no-fit. DeltaProduct’s S4/A5S_{4}/A_{5} at nh=2n_{h}{=}2 under group-element classification [10] is thereby reconciled, not contradicted: the law is representation-relative, and the task format selects the representation.

  3. 3.

    The mechanism (§5): from a verified-exact initialization with Wb=0W_{b}{=}0, training grows the additive path and destroys length generalization while −b-b controls are stable — and our registered prediction that the fit routes through btb_{t} was falsified by its own kill criterion: the additive path is parasitic. At law-minimal width the exact automaton is learned underneath it, and zeroing WbW_{b} at inference — no retraining — restores exact generalization in 18/2018/20 seeds across parity/S4S_{4}/S5S_{5}/A5A_{5}.

2 Setup

Model. A single recurrent layer with input-dependent Householder-product transitions [10]: ht=Rnh​(xt)​⋯​R1​(xt)​ht−1+bth_{t}=R_{n_{h}}(x_{t})\cdots R_{1}(x_{t})\,h_{t-1}+b_{t}, where each Ri​(xt)=I−2​ui​ui⊤R_{i}(x_{t})=I-2u_{i}u_{i}^{\top} (uiu_{i} a learned, normalized, input-dependent vector), d=112d{=}112, bt=Wb​etb_{t}=W_{b}e_{t} is the standard additive injection, and a per-step linear readout predicts the automaton state. The −b-b ablation deletes btb_{t} only: input then acts exclusively by selecting orthogonal transitions — the literal automaton form. Training: full 32-length sequences, 40 epochs, Adam, identical budget in every cell (Appendix A); no cell-specific tuning anywhere.

Tasks. Per-step tracking of a designated element’s position under generator sequences (vocabulary = 2 generators): parity (ℤ2\mathbb{Z}_{2}), mod-3 (ℤ3\mathbb{Z}_{3}), S4S_{4} (transposition + 4-cycle), A5A_{5} (3-cycle + 5-cycle; all-even), S5S_{5} (transposition + 5-cycle; non-solvable). Group closures and generator reflection lengths are asserted programmatically at runner start. Train L=32L{=}32, evaluate per-position to L=512L{=}512; GEN = median position-512 accuracy >0.9{>}0.9; shortcut = fits position 32 (>0.9{>}0.9) but <0.6{<}0.6 at 512; no-fit otherwise. Five seeds per primary cell; medians with [min,max][\min,\max].

Pre-registration. Grid, predictions, kill criteria, and decision rules were committed to the repository before any grid run existed (commit 2cf13e5), with verdicts recorded against them unchanged (84e21c8); the one exploratory antecedent (a 3-seed June pilot of the parity/S5S_{5} flip) is disclosed as such, and all its cells were re-run from scratch under this harness. Every number in this paper is a macro regenerated from the 202 run artifacts and byte-verified (Appendix A).

3 One deleted term flips shortcut to exact automaton

Table 1 is the paper’s central result; Figure 1 shows the per-position curves. With the additive path, every task that fits at all follows the shortcut profile: perfect at the training length, collapsed by 22–4×4\times beyond it (S5S_{5}: 1.001.00 at position 32 vs. 0.200.20 at 512). Deleting btb_{t} flips every such cell at or above the law width to exact generalization: parity 1.001.00, S4S_{4} 1.001.00, A5A_{5} 1.001.00, S5S_{5} 1.001.00 at position 512 (medians of 5; S5S_{5} and parity [1.00,1.00][1.00,1.00]). The GRU reference generalizes everywhere (as known); the negative-eigenvalue diagonal model cannot fit S5S_{5} (no order-5 real eigenvalue), reproducing the expressivity wall the Householder parameterization exists to cross.

4 The law: minimal nhn_{h} = generator reflection length, representation-relative

For a permutation generator PP, its reflection length in the defining representation is rank⁡(I−P)\operatorname{rank}(I-P): a transposition is 1 reflection, a 4-cycle 3, a 5-cycle 4. Because each step must realize a generator as a product of nhn_{h} Householder factors, representability requires nh≥maxg⁡rank⁡(I−Pg)n_{h}\geq\max_{g}\operatorname{rank}(I-P_{g}) in whatever faithful representation the model realizes. Empirically (Fig. 2), the −b-b models obey this law exactly in its defining-representation form: minimal generalizing nhn_{h} = 11 (parity), 33 (S4S_{4}), 44 (A5A_{5}), 44 (S5S_{5}), and every below-law cell is no-fit — e.g. S5S_{5} at nh=3n_{h}{=}3: 0.270.27 at position 32. For S5S_{5} the necessity is representation-independent: in every faithful representation of S5S_{5} the 5-cycle’s reflection length is 4 (checked across the irreducible representations), so nh=4n_{h}{=}4 is a true lower bound, which the nh=3n_{h}{=}3 cell confirms behaviorally.

For S4S_{4} and A5A_{5}, however, Siems et al. [10] report robust extrapolation at nh=2n_{h}{=}2 via their isomorphisms to rotation groups in S​O​(3)SO(3), where every element is a product of ≤2\leq 2 reflections. Our S4S_{4}/A5A_{5} cells at nh=2n_{h}{=}2 (and A5A_{5} at 3) are no-fit — both results are correct, and the reconciliation is the point: their format (group-element classification from a matrix state) admits the compact S​O​(3)SO(3) realization; our format (point tracking through a vector state with a per-step linear readout) pins the defining permutation representation, e.g. because the S​O​(3)SO(3) realization of S4S_{4} carries the four objects as unsigned diagonal axes whose sign ambiguity a linear readout cannot resolve. The law that survives both datasets is representation-relative: minimal per-step compute equals the generators’ reflection length in the cheapest faithful representation the task format admits. Task format is thus a hidden variable in state-tracking benchmarks — two papers can disagree about “the nhn_{h} needed for S4S_{4}” while both being right.

5 Mechanism: a parasitic attractor, discriminated by pre-registered arms

The additive route attracts even from the exact solution (E3). We construct exact solutions by hand (reflection programs whose composed factors are asserted equal to the generator permutation matrices; dead-axis factors implement identity), verify them (pre-training accuracy 1.001.00 at every probe position — a registered instrument gate), set Wb=0W_{b}{=}0, and train with the standard budget. Across 5 seeds on both parity and S5S_{5}: the probe additive-path magnitude ∥Wb​e∥\lVert W_{b}e\rVert grows steadily from 0 to 0.410.41 (parity) and 0.770.77 (S5S_{5}), the state norm inflates (impossible through the norm-preserving reflections), and position-512 accuracy falls to 0.500.50/0.230.23 — while the −b-b exact-init controls remain at 1.001.00/1.001.00 (Fig. 3). The additive pathway is not merely a basin that captures random initializations; under Adam + cross-entropy it destabilizes the exact global solution itself. A natural reading, stated as interpretation: orthogonal transitions cannot grow ∥h∥\lVert h\rVert, so the additive path is the only channel through which cross-entropy’s appetite for larger logits can express itself, and what it adds is position-dependent drift that is harmless in-domain and fatal out-of-distribution.

Our registered mechanism prediction was wrong; the kill criterion fired; the truth is better (P3/K2). We pre-registered that the +b+b shortcut’s in-domain fit routes through the additive path (prediction: zeroing WbW_{b} at inference collapses even position-32 accuracy). It does not — of the 4949 seeds across all +b+b cells that fit the training length, 4949 retain in-domain accuracy under Wb:=0W_{b}{:=}0 (e.g. S5S_{5}: 1.001.00 at position 32), so K2 fired as registered and the “leaky-counter route” story is withdrawn. What replaces it is stronger: at the law-minimal width, zeroing WbW_{b} at inference restores exact length generalization — parity 1.001.00 (55/5 seeds), S5S_{5} 0.990.99 (55/5), S4S_{4} 44/5, A5A_{5} 44/5. The trained “shortcut” model at minimal width contains the exact automaton: the reflections converge to it anyway, and the additive path merely conceals it. The same holds for the exact-init arms after being pulled off (Wb:=0W_{b}{:=}0 restores 1.001.00/1.001.00). The additive pathway is therefore parasitic in the precise sense that it neither performs the fit nor helps it; it grows because it is the cheapest confidence channel, and its growth both destabilizes training away from exact solutions and masks exact solutions once learned.

Boundaries (reported, not smoothed). The concealment result is width- and task-scoped: above the law width the freed reflection capacity co-adapts with the parasite and Wb:=0W_{b}{:=}0 restores only 11–22/5 seeds on parity (nh=2,4n_{h}{=}2,4); per-seed exceptions exist at law width (S4S_{4} 44/5, A5A_{5} 44/5). And mod-3 never locks: even −b-b, its median position-512 accuracy craters (0.000.00 at nh=4n_{h}{=}4 — systematic rotational drift, worse than chance, with individual seeds at 1.001.00). Notably this kills our own prior hypothesis (from the June pilot) that a transposition generator is what anchors exact solutions: A5A_{5}’s generators are all even — no transposition, no reflection among them — yet A5A_{5} locks exactly (1.001.00). What separates the locking tasks from mod-3 is open; we note only that mod-3 is abelian with a single continuously embeddable rotation generator, while the locking tasks force non-commuting discrete constraints.

Relation to rival mechanism accounts. The unexplored-states hypothesis [8] attributes length-generalization failure to training-time state coverage; our exact-init arms hold the initial solution (and hence its reachable state distribution) fixed and still observe the pull-off, so state coverage is not the operative variable here. Error-control theory [1] proves affine trackers accumulate uncorrectable error at inference; consistently, our +b+b models fail OOD — but the same weights with Wb:=0W_{b}{:=}0 are exact, and training grows btb_{t} from zero at an exact solution, so in this family the affine term is not a fixed design constraint that error dynamics must cope with but a learned liability that optimization actively acquires. The two accounts are complementary to ours (inference-time and data-distribution, respectively); the within-architecture ablation locates the training-time cause.

6 Discussion

What this changes. The expressivity-learnability gap in Householder linear RNNs has a locatable, causal, single-term source in this setting — and the failure it produces is not absence of the solution but occlusion of it. That reframes the practical question from “how do we make these models learn state tracking” to “how do we stop one pathway from first hijacking and then hiding what they already learn.” The minimal demonstration also yields an unreasonably cheap diagnostic: evaluating a trained model with its additive input path zeroed is a one-line probe for a concealed automaton, and at minimal width it doubles as a repair (18/2018/20 seeds across the four group tasks) — with the honest caveat that per-seed exceptions exist and the trick degrades off the minimal width.

What this does not claim. No universality: PD-SSM achieves exact S5S_{5} extrapolation with an additive term in a different architecture [14], and reveal-style supervision makes the additive path load-bearing and helpful [11]; our claims are scoped to Householder-product linear RNNs under standard supervision, where the transitions alone are expressive enough. No language-modeling prescription: in an LM the additive pathway carries content, and these tasks use input only to select transitions; “delete WbW_{b}” is a probe and a mechanism demonstration, not a training recipe. The law’s necessity side is representation-independent only where representation theory makes it so (S5S_{5}); elsewhere it is conditional on the format-pinned representation, which is exactly the point.

7 Limitations

Single architecture family, optimizer, and budget; d=112d{=}112; five tasks with two-generator vocabularies; 5 seeds per cell (counts over pp-values throughout); point-tracking format with per-step linear readout. Pre-registration is repository-native (commit-stamped in our own history, hashes in §2 and Appendix A), not third-party. The June pilot preceded registration and is disclosed; its mod-3 “flip” did not survive 5 seeds (median craters; reported in §5). The parasite account’s scope beyond this family — and what separates locking tasks from drifting ones — are open.

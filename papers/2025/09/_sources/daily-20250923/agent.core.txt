# Introduction



We are excited to introduce our **brand-new Travel Planning Assistant**, a powerful system built on a **Multi-Agent architecture** with robust  **real-world tool-calling capabilities**. It is designed to handle complex and dynamic itinerary planning tasks with high efficiency. Whether you’re preparing for a multi-city trip or an in-depth single-city exploration, it can deliver precise, actionable travel plans:


- **Daily schedules down to the hour**: covering transportation, accommodation, attractions, dining, and other key aspects.
- **Total budget estimates**: plan your expenses in advance.
- **Important travel reminders**: such as weather alerts, attraction regulations, and transportation updates.
- **Verified real-world data**: all routes and information are traceable and reliable.





# Key Features



## 🧠 Multi-Agent Architecture: Efficient Planning for Complex Trips



Travel planning is a **systematic task** involving transportation, attractions, lodging, dining, and more — each interconnected and mutually influential.  
A single agent cannot easily capture this complexity.  





To address this, we built a sophisticated **Multi-Agent collaboration framework** that efficiently manages dynamic interactions among modules.  
With intelligent coordination, the system adapts seamlessly to diverse travel scenarios:





- Single-city deep tours (e.g., Beijing)
- Specialized trips at large scenic areas (e.g., Qiandao Lake)
- Multi-city routes (e.g., the Xinjiang circuit)






## 🔍 Authenticity: Every Piece of Information is Traceable


The Travel Planning Assistant integrates **real-world third-party tools** (such as Amap, Fliggy and web search) to provide:





- Accurate flight, train, hotel, and attraction information.
- Real-time traffic updates, attraction opening status, and ticket prices.
- Precise calculations of distances and travel durations.
- Nearby restaurant recommendations.





During the generation of a **3-day itinerary**, the system may call tools more than **70 times**, ensuring authenticity and reliability in every plan.






## 🔁 Outstanding Planning and Reflection Capabilities


Powered by the strong **reasoning** and **self-reflection** abilities of the Qwen model, the system not only creates accurate query plans **proactively at the start** but also continuously **validates and optimizes** itineraries during execution.





**Planning Phase**:





<ul style="color: var(--character-primary-text);"><li>Predefines query targets according to the type of trip:<ul><li>Transportation methods</li><li>Attraction opening hours</li><li>Routes and distances between sites</li><li>Meal insertion points</li></ul></li></ul>



**Reflection Phase**:





<ul style="color: var(--character-primary-text);"><li>Continuously refines the plan based on <strong>real-time data</strong> from sub-agents:<ul><li>Late-night or next-day train arrivals → Automatically adjust attraction order to maximize efficiency.</li><li>Attractions closed on Mondays → Reschedule to another day and optimize the overall route.</li></ul></li></ul>

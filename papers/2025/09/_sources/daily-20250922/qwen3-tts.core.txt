**Qwen3-TTS-Flash** is a flagship text-to-speech model that supports multi-timbre, multi-lingual, and multi-dialect speech synthesis. It aims to produce natural and expressive speech and is available via [Qwen API](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/model-studio/qwen-tts).






Key Features:






* **Excellent Chinese-English Stability**: Qwen3-TTS-Flash achieves SOTA performance in Chinese and English stability on seed-tts-eval test set, surpassing SeedTTS, MiniMax, and GPT-4o-Audio-Preview.

* **Superior Multilingual Performance**: In terms of multilingual stability and timbre similarity, Qwen3-TTS-Flash achieves SOTA WER for Chinese, English, Italian, and French on MiniMax TTS multilingual test set, significantly lower than MiniMax, ElevenLabs, and GPT-4o-Audio-Preview. Speaker similarity in English, Italian, and French also significantly surpasses MiniMax, ElevenLabs, and GPT-4o-Audio-Preview.

* **High Expressiveness**: Qwen3-TTS-Flash delivers highly expressive and human-like voice timbres, providing stable and reliable speech output that faithfully follows the input text.

* **Rich Timbres and Languages**: Qwen3-TTS-Flash offers 17 distinct speaker options, each supporting 10 languages.

* **Multi-dialect Support**:  Qwen3-TTS-Flash supports dialect generation, including Mandarin, Hokkien, Wu dialect, Cantonese, Sichuanese, Beijing dialect, Nanjing dialect, Tianjin dialect, and Shaanxi dialect.

* **Tone Adaptation**: Trained on large-scale datasets, Qwen3-TTS-Flash can automatically adjust tone to match the input text.

* **Strong Robust**: Qwen3-TTS-Flash automatically processes complex texts by extracting key information, demonstrating robustness to complex and diverse formats.

* **Fast Generation**: Qwen3-TTS-Flash has very low first-packet latency with single-threaded first-packet latency as low as 97ms.

## Language Support



Qwen3-TTS-Flash enables multilingual speech generation for multi speakers through a unified model:





* **Chinese**: Mandarin, Hokkien, Wu dialect, Cantonese, Sichuanese, Beijing dialect, Nanjing dialect, Tianjin dialect, and Shaanxi dialect.

* **English**: British accent, American accent, and various regional English accents.

* **Other Languages**: French, German, Russian, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean.






## Performance



We conducted a comprehensive evaluation of Qwen3-TTS-Flash in terms of speech stability and timbre similarity, and it achieves SOTA performance across many results. Specifically, on the seed-tts-eval test set, Qwen3-TTS-Flash demonstrates SOTA stability for both Chinese and English, outperforming SeedTTS, MiniMax, and GPT-4o-Audio-Preview. Furthermore, on MiniMax TTS multilingual test set, Qwen3-TTS-Flash achieves SOTA WER for Chinese, English, Italian, and French, significantly lower than those of MiniMax, ElevenLabs, and GPT-4o-Audio-Preview. In terms of speaker similarity for English, Italian, and French, Qwen3-TTS-Flash also surpasses the aforementioned models, demonstrating remarkable performance in both multi-language stability and timbre similarity.

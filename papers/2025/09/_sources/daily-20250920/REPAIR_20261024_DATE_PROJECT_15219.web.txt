L207: ## Out-of-Sight Trajectory
L208: ### TPAMI Submission (Extended Version)
L209: 
L210: by 【90†Haichao Zhang†hai-chao-zhang.github.io】, 【91†Yi Xu†sites.google.com】, 【92†Yun Fu†www1.ece.neu.edu】.
L211: 
L212: * * *
L213: 
L214: Pytorch implementation of the TPAMI extended version of 【93†"OOSTraj: Out-of-Sight Trajectory Prediction With Vision-Positioning Denoising"†arxiv.org】.
L215: 
L216: * * *
L217: Out-of-sight trajectory prediction is critical for real-world applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and surveillance, where agents may become occluded or move beyond the field of view. While previous methods rely on clean visual data, they fail to address challenges from noisy or partial sensor observations.
L218: 
L219: Our TPAMI extension builds upon the CVPR 2024 conference version and presents several key improvements:
L220:   * Generalized prediction for diverse agent types, including both pedestrians and vehicles.
L221:   * A refined Vision-Positioning Denoising (VPD) framework with improved feature fusion and uncertainty-aware projection.
L222:   * Expanded benchmarks on occluded trajectory prediction with comparisons to Kalman/Particle filters and deep learning models.
L223:   * In-depth ablations and a newly added discussion on limitations and future directions (e.g., dynamic cameras, 3D scene reconstruction).
L224: By robustly denoising trajectories from noisy sensor data and mapping them into coherent visual space, our method significantly boosts performance under out-of-view scenarios. This work sets a new standard for out-of-sight trajectory reasoning.

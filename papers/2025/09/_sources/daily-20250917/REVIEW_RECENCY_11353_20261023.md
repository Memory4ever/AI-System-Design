## Central measurement conflict

The initial topK documents have by construction the K *smallest assigned years*. For any permutation of the same100 items, the mean year in the new topK cannot be below that original minimum. Thus YS(K)>=0 is forced by the assignment, even for a reranker that ignores the meaning of dates and merely changes the list. A strictly positive top-year shift or one-sample test vs0 does not by itself identify a directional recency preference. The same extremal property makes the bottom decile necessarily nonpositive. AARS/ALRS are absolute displacement, also directionless. Do not reinterpret their nonzero t-tests as a test of newer-over-older causality.

Independent finite illustration, not an author experiment: assign years1926,1927,1928,1929 along an initial rank4 list, K2. Across all24 uniformly random reorderings, topK year shift has min0, mean+1, max+2 years, although the new ranking is independent of timestamps. This suffices to refute the claimed directional interpretation of the statistic alone; it does not prove the original rerankers ignored timestamps.

The second pass also reranks a list that is itself a first-pass LLM output. The read protocol does not give a matched second-pass no-date control, equal-date/neutral prefix, randomized/role-swapped date assignment or inverse date mapping, so ordinary non-idempotence, changed prefix/token positions and the intended date semantics are not separated by listwise displacement. Temperature0 is not a proof of reranker idempotence or whole-service determinism. Middle-decile patterns are not an independently randomized direction control because dates remain coupled to the first ranking and shared sliding windows.

Pairwise preserves passage bodies and records concrete flips under a combined old/new-prefix treatment, so the narrow sensitivity evidence remains valuable. But selecting the initially preferred passage to receive the old date, without matched no-date repeat, both-new/both-old/neutral and swapped treatments, does not isolate recency from all prefix/threshold/repetition effects or show how often actual relevance is harmed. It also cannot independently extend to the three proprietary models absent from pairwise. The manuscript itself limits pairwise scope and fixes window size; it does not supply these missing controls elsewhere in S3-6. No inference that every flip is spurious or that the empirical tables are fabricated.

No NDCG/answer support/temporal-intent benefit-vs-harm evaluation is supplied in these necessary domains. Some real queries properly depend on freshness; artificial publication date is not factual validity, and a common coarse human relevance grade does not certify the prefix leaves full task meaning unchanged. Date perturbation is enough for this proposed research question, not enough to sign all-query timestamp irrelevance or a universal mitigation.

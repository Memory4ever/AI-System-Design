In OptiMA, the non-shareable plugins are treated as the critical resources of the system, and the purpose of CC is to regulate their use.\nL155: Multi-version concurrency control methods do not serve this purpose, since it is not possible to keep multiple versions of most plugins (cite71†Korf, 2009 ).

L560@P5: 4 IMPLEMENTATION
L561@P5: Our goal in formulating SnapStream is to bridge the implementation gap between KV cache compression and LLM
L562@P5: deployments, particularly for frameworks that use static
L563@P5: graphs. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach by
L564@P5: applying SnapStream to a real deployment of a SoTA reasoning LLM, DeepSeek-R1-0528 (DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025a),
L565@P5: on the SN40L accelerator. In this section, we describe the
L566@P5: kernel fusion and tensor sharding decisions, known as a
L567@P5: “mapping”, for a deployment of DeepSeek-R1-0528 on a
L568@P5: single node of 16×SN40Ls. Separate mappings are created
L569@P5: for prefill and decoding that optimize for each phase’s respective SLOs. We also show how SnapStream integrates
L570@P5: seamlessly into these mappings.

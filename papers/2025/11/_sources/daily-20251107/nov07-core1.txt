Model Introduction\nL171: \nL172: Kimi K2 is a state-of-the-art mixture-of-experts (MoE) language model with 32 billion activated parameters and 1 trillion total parameters. Trained with the Muon optimizer, Kimi K2 achieves exceptional performance across frontier knowledge, reasoning, and coding tasks while being meticulously optimized for agentic capabilities.\nL173: ### Key Features\nL174: \nL175:   * Large-Scale Training: Pre-trained a 1T parameter MoE model on 15.5T tokens with zero training instability.\nL176:   * MuonClip Optimizer: We apply the Muon optimizer to an unprecedented scale, and develop novel optimization techniques to resolve instabilities while scaling up.\nL177:   * Agentic Intelligence: Specifically designed for tool use, reasoning, and autonomous problem-solving.\nL178: ### Model Variants\nL179: \nL180:   * Kimi-K2-Base: The foundation model, a strong start for researchers and builders who want full control for fine-tuning and custom solutions.\nL181:   * Kimi-K2-Instruct: The post-trained model, best for drop-in, general-purpose chat and agentic experiences. It is a reflex-grade model without long thinking.\nL182: \nL183: cite81†Image: Evaluation Results L184: ## 2.

CVPR 2025 - Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Conference
The IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition 2025
Wed June 11th - Sun June 15th, 2025
at the Music City Center, Nashville TN
firstback
The IEEE / CVF Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Conference (CVPR) is the premier annual
computer vision event comprising the main conference and several co-located workshops and short courses.   With
its high quality and low cost, it provides an exceptional value for students, academics and industry
researchers.
